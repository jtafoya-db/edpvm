The official U.S. government site-identification banner. Goes at the very top of every page, above the header.

```jsx
<Banner />
<Banner tld=".mil" />
```

Collapsed: small flag + "An official website of the United States government" + a "Here's how you know" toggle. Expanded: two columns explaining `.gov` ownership and HTTPS security. Assets are inlined, so it works anywhere with no image paths.
